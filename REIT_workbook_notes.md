## How to read the NDCF / debt table
In Indian REITs the Trust funds its SPVs partly via **shareholder loans**; distributions come back as interest, dividend, and repayment of those loans (return of capital). Interest+dividend = real income; return-of-capital = unitholders getting their own money back. The 'Return-of-capital % of distribution' row flags how much of each year's payout is *not* yield. Block B separates **external** debt (banks/NCDs/LRD - the cash-consuming, leverage-driving debt) from the intra-group **shareholder loans**.
